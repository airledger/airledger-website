Privacy

Your details, kept simple

What we collect on this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Air Ledger. You can contact us about anything on this page at hello@airledger.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Waiting list. Your name, business name, email, number of engineers, the system you use now, and the page or link that brought you here.
  • Demo requests. The form opens your own email app. We only receive what you choose to send.
  • Visitor statistics. Anonymous counts of page views and where visits come from. No cookies, and nothing that identifies you.

Why we use it

To tell you when Air Ledger opens, hold your founding member place, and reply to demo requests. We rely on your consent when you join the waiting list, and on our legitimate interest in replying to messages you send us.

We will never sell your details or pass them to anyone for their own marketing.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores the waiting list on servers in the EU (Stockholm).
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, which means your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies.

How long we keep it

Waiting list details are kept until you ask us to remove them, or for 12 months after paid plans open if you don't sign up, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted at any time. Email hello@airledger.co.uk and we'll do it within a month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website doesn't set any cookies. The Air Ledger app at app.airledger.co.uk uses only what it needs to keep you signed in.
