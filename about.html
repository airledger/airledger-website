About

Built by an AC engineer

Air Ledger wasn't dreamt up in an office. It was built inside a working air conditioning and electrical business, by the person who goes out on the jobs.

Why it exists

Running an AC business means keeping track of hundreds of units across dozens of sites: what's installed where, how much refrigerant is in it, when it's next due, and who looked at it last. Most of that lived on paper, in spreadsheets and in people's heads.

The big job management systems could do it, but they cost a fortune per engineer and took months to set up. The cheap trade apps were easy, but they didn't know what a refrigerant charge or a leak check was.

So we built our own. Every unit gets a label, every visit is logged, the F-gas sums do themselves, and invoices are ready the moment the customer signs. It worked so well that it became Air Ledger.

What we believe

  • Simple beats clever. If an engineer can't use it with gloves on in a plant room, it's not finished.
  • Fair pricing. One price for the business, not a bill that grows every time you hire.
  • Your data is yours. Export everything, any time. No lock-in.
  • Built with the trade. New features come from real jobs and real customers, not a roadmap meeting.
